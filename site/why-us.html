Under the hood

Accuracy is a system.

Not one clever trick — layered matching, tamper checks, reports you can defend, and people who answer.

Layered matching

Every check runs the live web — 14B indexed pages — plus a large offline document base. One pass, no "lite mode" that quietly skips sources.

Disguises don't work

Homoglyph detection catches look-alike character swaps; sentence-structure analysis catches reshuffled paraphrase. Hidden copying reads as copying.

Reports worth keeping

Sentence-level highlights with clickable sources. Store reports in your profile, download the PDF for a supervisor, or delete them for good.

Support that answers

Real humans around the clock — typical reply in about 4 hours, any day of the year. No chatbot maze.
