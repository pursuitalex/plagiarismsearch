Our trained specialists will scrutinize your piece of writing thoroughly to detect any possible mistakes. When analyzing papers, close attention is paid to the following aspects.

Content

Our specialists always comment on the points that require some modifications. They also state whether the piece of writing is quality enough to catch readers’ attention. Additionally, they ensure the text is comprehensible.

EXCERPT · EDITOR’S NOTE

The results of the study were very good and showed that a lot of different factors played a part in the outcome, which is something that future researchers should probably look at more.

STYLE Vague quantifiers weaken a findings paragraph. Name the factors and state the effect size.

Grammar

The errors available in the text will be highlighted. The explanations going alongside the corrections will help you see the made mistakes and avoid them when producing your next work.

Research

Our experts will make comments on the validity of the carried out investigation. They will state whether the conducted research is extensive enough to cover the subject.

Organizational mode

Our pros will make a thorough analysis of your paper structure and spell out if any changes are required.

Style

Our editors will assess your writing style and explain whether all stylistic standards are met.
