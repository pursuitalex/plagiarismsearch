What is included

Here is what you get by subscribing to a VIP Package

Our company offers its customers VIP support packages, which are available on either a monthly or annual subscription basis — so you can experience the benefits for one month before deciding whether to subscribe for a year.

An abundance of submissions for a decent price

This is an excellent chance if you need to check more than 1 million words monthly, aiming to eradicate any kind of plagiarism. Depending on what you need as a student, teacher, professor, or an independent writer, you may select the package you are most comfortable with.

A VIP level of support

Our VIP offer provides the customer with a special individualistic approach. We have arranged a separate phone line for VIP customers — our support personnel will be on hand 24×7 to assist you.

Own storage of text messages, reports, etc.

We will automatically assign your own area or repository. Our system saves reports for up to thirty days, but all VIP customers keep their personal databases for a longer period.

A personal client manager assigned to the customer

Once you subscribe, we will assign a customer manager who will deal with any inquiries that do not concern the technical aspects of our service. Your manager also communicates relevant news, system updates, and any discounts you may be eligible for.

Since checking for plagiarism is one of the last things that is done before a paper is submitted, our VIP service and the assistance of a personal manager will be available to you to resolve any problems that might arise.
