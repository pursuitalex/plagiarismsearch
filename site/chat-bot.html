Novelties

Let us introduce our novelties:

Telegram’s bot plagiarism checker and Viber’s bot plagiarism checker.

Straight from your messenger

From now on, you can check the required text for plagiarism straight from your messenger. Everything you need to find is PlagiarismSearch.com on Telegram or Viber, and then insert the text that has to be checked (or upload the necessary document).

Billions of web pages, then a PDF

PlagiarismSearch.com will instantly start plagiarism detection against billions of web pages. After the detection is over, you will get notification with the plagiarism detection result. Besides, to review the result fully, you will obtain a detailed report in a PDF format as well as a link to a report on PlagiarismSearch.com.

Your history stays put

Your reports will remain on your plagiarism-scanning history (unless you delete them). Hence, you will always be able to view Plagiarism Checker results again (both on the app and on our website).

One account, one balance

For your convenience, we recommend amalgamating your account on the website PlagiarismSearch.com into the one on your messenger. In this case, you will have a single account with the common balance and reports history.
