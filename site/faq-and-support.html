A plagiarism report in the pdf format or a Google Docs add-on has a specific visual representation with words and sentences highlighted in different colors. A report on a plagiarism-free document has no highlights. However, if there is at least some minor plagiarism, it will be highlighted in the similarity report with a particular color.

The text highlighted in green

The text in green color means that the document contains approximately 15% of plagiarized content. The result is obtained via comparison of the required sentences with the web resources.

The text highlighted in yellow

The text in yellow color means that the document contains approximately 30 - 50 % of plagiarized content. Even if there are 65% of secondary similarities, the text may be yellow provided that the initial coincidence does not exceed 50%.

The text highlighted in red

The text in red color means that the document contains more than 75 % of plagiarized content. It is the highest plagiarism percentage in specific sentences which greatly affects the overall similarity score in the document.

The text highlighted in blue

Blue shades are not related to plagiarism in the document. Instead, they highlight the used bibliography or references.

The text highlighted in purple

Purple shades show in-text citations. Formatted properly, they do not change the plagiarism percentage.

Readability score checker FAQ

Readability score
0100
WordsSentencesSyllablesCharacters

In brief, the level of readability shows how easy or complicated it is to comprehend the text in writing. It is actually the score given for the quality of writing. Communicating a certain message without any sensory signals or clues of the body language, the writer hopes to make the ideas clear for the readers and avoid misunderstandings. It is essential to take into account all the elements that determine the level of readability: the word choice, sentence structure, length of sentences and paragraphs, and even the average number of syllables in a word. Processing of the provided information is much easier provided that the readability is high.

You definitely want your readers find the written text easy and understandable. However, you need a certain measurement to know whether your language needs improvements.

Checking readability, you will have special metrics for calculating the score and introducing changes if needed to match what you have written with your target audience. Depending on the level of readability, you can make your text suitable for the students, professionals in a particular sphere, or business.

Our readability checker assesses the uploaded files or copy-pasted texts with the help of a Flesch Grade Level formula. Our tool uses a special Flesch Kincaid Calculator to specify the readability score of the created content and also determine whether there are any flaws in your completed work. It counts the words, sentences, syllables, and characters in the provided text, analyzes them and connects them to the Flesch Kincaid Grade Level formula.

It is valuable for the writers not only to know a general readability score of the text, but also to get professional guidance on how it can be improved and adjusted for the target readers. The checker will highlight excessively complex or vague sentences that may confuse the audience. It will also let you know whether there is any wordiness, excessive use of passive sentences, tautological structures, redundant terminology, and other issues.

Drag and drop the needed file, simply attach it, or use the options of inserting it from Dropbox, OneDrive, and by URL. Double check whether the type of the file is supported by the software:

Microsoft Word (.docx, .doc), OpenOffice (.odt), Microsoft Excel (.xls, .xlsx), Adobe (.pdf), Rich text (.rtf), Plain text (.txt), Extensible Markup Language (.xml), data file (.org), e-book (.epub), HyperText Markup Language (), and other. The maximum file size for checking is 24 MB. In some cases, it is easier to copy and paste the text for readability checking into a corresponding wondow.

The customers can make use of two options that our Readability Score provides. A free check gives an easy and fast measurement of the level with no further explanations or guidance on how to improve the text and make it suitable for particular readers.

As the Flesch reading ease test forms the analysis, the tool generates a readability score in the range from 0 to 100. This numerical score will help you understand how complex your document is. The lower the score, the more complicated it is to read your text.

The score from 0 to 10 means that only single-discipline experts can gain an insight into the content of this writing. It requires the audience highly competent in this particular sphere and it will be extremely ambiguous for common readers. On the other hand, the score from 90 to 100 means that even the readers from elementary school will understand the text.

In most cases, it is recommendable to adjust writing to the score of minimum 60. In that case, the text will be understandable for most of the people, even if they have got only the eight-grade education. That is approximately the level of understanding suitable for a 13-year-old. Still, it does not mean that the text will sound bog-standard. Instead, it implies that the readers will not have to spend too much energy to understand your arguments.

Our Readability Score Checker offers an additional option of advanced checking with further interpretation and recommendations concerning possible improvements in readability of a particular text. The customers get access to this option, paying for the package of services.
