Human vs AI

Identify human vs
AI-generated text.

The rapid growth of generative AI in education, business communication, recruitment and digital media has created a real need for reliable tools to verify text authenticity. Our detector determines whether a document was written by a human, generated by AI, or contains a mix of both.

Trained on extensive datasets of human-written and AI-generated content, the model recognises outputs from today's most advanced large language models — including GPT-5, Claude, Gemini, LLaMA 2 and Mistral — down to subtle stylistic signatures.

AI detection is a valuable indicator rather than a final verdict. Accuracy is highest on complete documents — short excerpts give the model too little to work with.
