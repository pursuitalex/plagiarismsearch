Why it matters

Why plagiarism
checking matters

Plagiarism checking is a core part of academic integrity. A quick online check helps you credit sources correctly, avoid unintentional similarities, and submit a clean, original paper. Teachers and universities expect proper referencing — verifying your work before submission saves time and reduces risk.

Students have to work on too many assignments simultaneously, and they might forget to put quotation marks around a direct quote or to paraphrase a sentence properly. Submitting a plagiarism-free paper is as important as following the instructions.

Good habits

Tips for students to avoid plagiarism

Paraphrase properly

Rewrite the idea in your own words, not by swapping a few synonyms.

Cite every source

Credit each source and keep one consistent citation style throughout.

Mark direct quotes

Use quotation marks whenever the wording is not your own.

Check before you submit

Run a final plagiarism check — most similarities are unintentional.
