The honest picture

Why plagiarism occurs anyway

Most of the time plagiarism happens due to confusion regarding the assignment, improper citing of the source, and rarely for the purpose of stealing someone else's work.

Confusion about the assignment

The most common cause. The requirements were never clear enough for the writer to know what counts as borrowed material.

Improper citing of the source

The source is credited somewhere, but not the way the passage itself required — so the text still reads as unattributed.

Stealing someone else's work

The rare case — and in both the academic and the digital world, the one that can destroy a career in a moment.

By citing properly and following basic rules of courtesy and integrity, we at PlagiarismSearch believe we can educate a new, honest generation of conscious Internet users. Most plagiarism happens unintentionally — therefore an extra check before final submission could be a life savior.
