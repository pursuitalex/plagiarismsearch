Climate change and the economics of food

The economic implications of climate change extend beyond environmental damage and touch every part of modern agricultural systems.

Recent studies have shown that rising global temperatures correlate with decreased yields in rain-fed regions, and that the effect compounds where irrigation is unavailable.

However, smallholder farmers in West Africa have adapted through diversified cropping patterns and drought-resistant millet varieties.

What the models leave out

Most projections treat adaptation as a fixed parameter rather than a decision made season by season under uncertainty.

Field data from recent seasons supports this reading across tropical zones, though the sample remains too small to generalise from with confidence.

Further work should separate the price effect from the yield effect before either is used to guide policy.

How It Works

How to Check a PowerPoint for Plagiarism

You do not need to copy every slide into a separate document. Upload your presentation, run the check, and review the matching text and its sources.

  1. 01

    Upload your PPT or PPTX

    Choose the presentation from your device or drag it into the checker. The supported file upload flow is the same as on other PlagiarismSearch checker pages.

  2. 02

    Run the plagiarism check

    PlagiarismSearch processes supported text from your presentation and compares it with the search sources available for your check.

  3. 03

    Review your report

    Look at the highlighted passages, inspect the sources, and decide whether a citation, quotation, or revision is needed.

What Your Results Mean

Understand the Result Before You Make a Decision

Similarity is not a plagiarism verdict

A matching passage can be a quotation, a citation, commonly used wording, or material that needs attribution. Review the text and its source before deciding what the match means.

AI writing analysis is separate

AI-writing indicators address a different question from source similarity. An AI result does not by itself establish plagiarism, misconduct, or who authored the work.

Your Document & Report

What Happens to Your PowerPoint After the Check?

Processing a presentation, keeping a generated report, and adding a document to Storage are different actions. It is important not to confuse them.

  1. 01

    Your presentation

    Your uploaded PowerPoint is processed to perform the check using the sources and settings you select. A check does not by itself mean the file has been added to your reusable Storage collection.

  2. 02

    Your report

    The generated report may remain available in your account, depending on your account and service settings. You can review the matching passages and their sources in the report.

  3. 03

    Optional Storage

    Where Storage is available, adding documents to it is a separate account-controlled workflow. Storage availability and settings may vary by plan and account.
